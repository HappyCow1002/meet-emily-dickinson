“I dwell in Possibility —”

Emily Elizabeth Dickinson was born in Amherst, Massachusetts, on December 10, 1830. She spent most of her life in and around the family Homestead, where poems, letters, gardens, and a vivid inner world filled her days.

She loved tending plants, reading, writing letters, and exchanging ideas with friends and family. Though she wrote nearly 1,800 poems, only a small number appeared in print while she was alive. Her unconventional dashes, unexpected rhymes, and vivid images became celebrated after her death in 1886.

Her poetry often examines hope, nature, loss, faith, joy, and the strange beauty of ordinary moments.
